Privacy Policy

This policy describes how the game “Чехарда / Leap-frog” (the “App”) handles user data.

  • We do not collect personal data. The App does not ask for your name, e-mail, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.
  • No ads, no analytics. The App contains no advertising SDKs, analytics or tracking services and does not use the Advertising ID.
